# Privacy

**Last updated:** 26 September 2026

Primus respects your privacy. The app is designed to work without collecting or tracking your personal information or usage data.

## Data collection

Primus does **not collect, store on our servers, transmit, or share**:

- Personal information
- Usage or analytics data
- Tracking data
- Game scores or player data for analytics or advertising

We do not use your data to track you across apps or websites.

## Your game data

The game information you enter is used to provide the app's features, such as tracking players, scores, rounds, and game history.

Your game data is backed up to your **private iCloud account** so it can be preserved with your Apple account and available across your devices where supported. This data is stored in your iCloud and is not collected or accessed by us.

You can disable iCloud backup for Primus in your device's **Settings**.

## Third-party services

Primus does not use third-party analytics or tracking services to collect information about how you use the app.

## Changes to this policy

If our privacy practices change, this policy will be updated and the new version will be made available at this URL.
