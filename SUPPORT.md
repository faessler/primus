# Support

Thanks for using the app. This app is designed to make keeping score for board games quick and simple, with a few handy tools for game night.

## Getting started

1. Create a new game and enter the game name.
2. Add your players.
3. Start scoring. Tap **+** or **−** on a player's card to change their score.
4. Use the game menu **(...)** for additional options and tools.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I enter a custom score?

Tap the number on a player's score card. A sheet will appear where you can enter a custom amount to add or subtract.

### Can I play over multiple rounds?

Yes. Use **Next round** to continue the game. The current total is shown on each player's card. In **Table view**, you can see all scores round by round.

### How do I change the player order?

By default, players are shown in the order they were created.

- To manually arrange player cards, long-press a card and drag it up or down.
- You can also use **(...) → Sort by** to change the sorting method.

### How do I play a game where the lowest score wins?

Open the **(...)** menu and enable **Reverse scoring**. This is useful for games such as Uno where the player with the lowest score wins.

### How do I choose a starting player?

In an in-progress game, open **(...)** and choose **Choose starting player**. The app will select a player at random.

### Where can I find the dice and hourglass?

You can find both tools in the **(...)** menu on the home screen or in an in-progress game.

To remove a die, long-press it.

### Can I change a player's color or photo?

Yes. Tap a player's card or avatar to open their player view. From there, you can change their color or avatar image and see their personal statistics.

### Can I delete a player?

Players cannot be deleted manually. A player is removed automatically once all games they were part of have been deleted.

### What happens when I end a game?

Ending a game closes it and shows the final statistics. Closed games are moved to the **History** tab.

If you ended a game accidentally, open it and use the **(...)** menu to reopen it.

### How do I delete a game?

In-progress and past games can be deleted by swiping the game to the left.

### Can I reuse previous game names?

Yes. When creating a game, previously entered game names are suggested for quick selection.

To remove a suggested game name, long-press it.
